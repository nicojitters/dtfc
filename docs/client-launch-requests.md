## For Lola — content and operational decisions

### 1. Real support email address

We need a real email address that visitors can reach you at. It appears in a few places: as the &ldquo;please email us at&rdquo; fallback whenever a form isn&rsquo;t configured yet, as the target of the &ldquo;Email us about donating&rdquo; button on the Donate page, and in a couple of other &ldquo;get in touch&rdquo; contexts. Right now it&rsquo;s a placeholder (`hello@dtfc.example`) that goes nowhere.

**Format:** just the email address — e.g. `hello@dtfc.org` or `contact@wetellstories.org` — whichever inbox you actually want messages to land in.

### 2. Real web address (domain)

The domain the site will live at once we go public. Right now the site builds against a placeholder host, and that placeholder appears in the &ldquo;canonical URL&rdquo; that search engines and social-media previews use.

**Format:** the domain — e.g. `dtfc.org` or `developmentaltheatre.org`. If DT:FC already has a domain purchased, just send us the name; we&rsquo;ll point it at the site. If you haven&rsquo;t bought one yet, let us know and we can recommend a registrar.

### 3. Zeffy donation link

The Donate page currently shows a &ldquo;coming soon&rdquo; badge and an &ldquo;Email us about donating&rdquo; button as a fallback. As soon as you send us the Zeffy link (through We Tell Stories, the fiscal sponsor), we swap the button to a real Donate call-to-action and remove the badge.

**Format:** the full Zeffy URL, e.g. `https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/xxxxxxxx`. If Zeffy gives you both a link and an embeddable widget option, we can use either — the link is simpler.

### 4. Where Ask Shakespeare submissions should go

Right now, when someone submits a question through the &ldquo;Ask Shakespeare&rdquo; form, the submission collects in a dashboard we control. We need to know: which inbox should those questions actually forward to? Whoever is going to review submissions and write the responses.

**Format:** just the email address. Can be the same as #1 (support email) or a different one dedicated to Shakespeare submissions — your call.

### 5. Membership tiers content

The Membership page currently shows a &ldquo;coming with our next content release&rdquo; badge and only offers an interest signup form. When you&rsquo;re ready to publish tiers, we replace the placeholder with real content and remove the badge.

**Format:** for each membership tier, please send:
- **Tier name** (e.g. &ldquo;Player,&rdquo; &ldquo;Facilitator,&rdquo; &ldquo;Steward&rdquo;)
- **Price** (annual or monthly? if both, what does each cost?)
- **What&rsquo;s included** (a short list — access to workshops, printed manual, listing in the community, etc.)
- **How someone joins** (do they click through to Zeffy? A separate signup form? Something else?)

We&rsquo;ll turn this into a clean tier grid on the page. If tiers aren&rsquo;t finalized yet, the placeholder is fine to leave up — this isn&rsquo;t a launch-blocker.

### 6. Testimonials moderation policy

The Testimonials page has a &ldquo;share your story&rdquo; form and a display area (currently empty). When people submit stories, do you want:

- **Moderated** — every submission comes to you for review before it appears on the site, and you tell us which to publish (we then add it manually)? OR
- **Open with light editing** — submissions post automatically with light copy-editing, and you can remove anything that shouldn&rsquo;t be there afterward?

Most nonprofits pick moderated for launch and revisit later. Your call.

**Format:** just tell us &ldquo;moderated&rdquo; or &ldquo;open,&rdquo; plus any editorial guidelines you want us to apply (e.g. no last names, no political content, that kind of thing).

### 7. Newsletter service ownership

For the newsletter signup forms (footer, landing tile, and the Newsletters page), we set up an account with a form-collection service called **Formspree** so submissions could start being caught during development. Two questions:

- **Do you want us to hand off ownership of that account** so DT:FC controls it long-term? OR
- **Do you already have a preferred newsletter service** (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Brevo, etc.) that we should route the forms to instead?

Either works. If you have a preferred service, please send us the account owner email so we can coordinate the swap.

## For Desirae — brand assets

### 8. Favicon

The small square icon that appears in the browser tab, on the browser bookmarks bar, and on iOS/Android when someone saves the site to their home screen. Currently a placeholder.

**Format:** ideally an SVG (scales cleanly at every size). If SVG isn&rsquo;t practical, we need at minimum:
- `favicon.ico` at 32×32 pixels
- One PNG at 180×180 pixels (for iOS home-screen icon)

The simplest design tends to work best — something recognizable at 16×16 pixels. If DT:FC has a mark or monogram, that&rsquo;s usually the right choice.

### 9. Social sharing image

The image that appears when someone shares a link to any DT:FC page on Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord, iMessage, etc. Currently a placeholder.

**Format:** a single 1200×630 pixel PNG (or JPG) that visually represents DT:FC — typically a combination of the DT:FC wordmark plus a warm background color from the site palette (the ivory, clay, or teal tones). The same image works for every page share, so it needs to be general-purpose.

If you want different images for different sections (Theatre Games vs. Shakespeare vs. Children&rsquo;s Theatre, etc.), that&rsquo;s a nice-to-have we can add post-launch.

### 10. DT:FC logo

The DT:FC logo/wordmark that appears in the header of every page. Currently a placeholder.

**Format:** SVG preferred (again — scales cleanly). If not SVG, a PNG at least 200 pixels wide with a transparent background. The site renders it in a small circular container in the top-left, so a design that reads well at ~40 pixels tall matters more than a design that looks great at 400 pixels.

## Joint (both) — optional polish for launch

### 11. Seed testimonials (optional)

If you have any existing testimonials — quotes from Players, Facilitators, teachers, parents, or partner theatres — send them now and we&rsquo;ll seed the Testimonials page with them instead of shipping the empty state. Otherwise the page will populate over time as submissions arrive.

**Format:** for each testimonial, we need:
- The quote itself (up to about 3 sentences)
- Who said it (name + role, e.g. &ldquo;Jane Doe, Facilitator&rdquo;)
- Location (city + state, optional)
- Whether they&rsquo;ve given permission to publish

### 12. Past newsletter issues (optional)

If you have back issues of the DT:FC newsletter that you&rsquo;d like on the site archive, send them and we&rsquo;ll add them. Right now the archive has one issue as a starting point.

**Format:** the newsletter body itself in any editable format (Word doc, Google Doc, plain text, or even a PDF we&rsquo;ll transcribe). For each issue we need the publish date, an issue number, and the content.
