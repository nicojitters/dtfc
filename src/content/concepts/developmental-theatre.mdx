## What Is Developmental Theatre?

Using theatre techniques to develop well-rounded and whole human beings fearless in their creativity. Our values are represented in everything we offer. Our acclaimed reputation and experience with multifaceted approaches to learning celebrates:

- Being a fun, vital, fulfilling intersection of education and the arts
- Unlocking potential by encouraging expanding and exploring individual skills in a supportive, collaborative group environment
- Providing daily theatre-based experiments to expand five natural competencies yielding fearless creativity
