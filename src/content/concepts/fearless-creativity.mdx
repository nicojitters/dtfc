## Genesis

Roger Holzberg ignited the concept of Fearless Creativity. He fearlessly lives a creative life — Founder of ReImagine Well, actor, playwright, professor, one-time Disney Imagineer, athlete, and supporter of many individual artists and groups.

Roger was a participant of the original theatre experiments in the 1970s and 80s at the University of Colorado that created Developmental Theatre. In 2021 he visited founders Chuck and Lola Wilcox and told them: "What we learned then was to be creative… no, Fearlessly Creative."

Roger teaches that the first action of Fearless Creativity is: **"Say Yes, and…"**

Once you’ve said Yes, focused exploration follows.

## Four Key Concepts

- **Creativity** — we all have it; Fearless Creativity is learning how to access it without flinching.
- **Constraints** — the resistance that sparks creativity. Limitations point the way to "Fearless."
- **Cohesion** — how well a group works together: Low, Medium, or High. Match the game to the group.
- **Competencies** — train with Theatre Games.

## The Five Competencies of Fearless Creativity

- **Physical Expression** — experience it through the body.
- **Vocal Expression** — communicate it with the voice.
- **Context Awareness** — the who, when, and where; the importance of collaboration and cooperation.
- **Risk Assessment and Management** — the who and how; constraints point the way to "Fearless."
- **Resilience** — discovering the What through trials and experience. There is no failure, only learning.

## What Fearless Creativity is Not

Fearless Creativity is not *Foolish Creativity*. The competency of Risk Assessment and Management sits at the center — it is the capacity to assess a situation clearly, make a considered decision, and then act with full commitment. Fear is not eliminated; it is acknowledged and moved through.
