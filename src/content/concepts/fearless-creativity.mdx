## Genesis

[Roger Holzberg](/legacy/timeline/) ignited the concept of Fearless Creativity. He fearlessly lives a creative life — Founder of ReImagine Well, actor, playwright, professor, one-time Disney Imagineer, athlete, and supporter of many individual artists and groups.

Roger was a participant of the original theatre experiments in the 1970&ndash;80s at the University of Colorado (C.U.) that created Developmental Theatre. In 2021 he visited founders Chuck and Lola Wilcox and told them: &ldquo;What we learned then was to be creative&hellip; no, Fearlessly Creative.&rdquo;

Roger teaches that the first action of Fearless Creativity is: **&ldquo;Say Yes And&hellip;&rdquo;**

Once you&rsquo;ve said Yes, focused exploration follows.

## Four Key Concepts

- **Creativity** — we all have it; how to be Fearless
- **Constraints** — the resistance that sparks creativity (see [Constraints](/resource-center/plot/#constraints))
- **Cohesion** — how well a group works together: <Concept id="cohesion" /> Low, Medium, or High
- **Competencies** — train with <Concept id="theatre-games" />

## Using the Five Theatre Games Competencies

- **Physical Expression** — experience it
- **Vocal Expression** — selling it
- **Context Awareness** — the who, when, and where; importance of collaboration and cooperation
- **Risk Assessment and Management** — the who and how; constraints point the way to &ldquo;Fearless&rdquo;
- **Resilience** — discovering the What through trials and experiences
