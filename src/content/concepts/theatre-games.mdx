## Theatre Games: What Are They?

Theatre games are an action-packed, performance-based method for individual and group development. Using entertaining dramatic techniques activates individual expression as each player steps outside ordinary responses. The process expands the five natural competencies necessary for Fearless Creativity. Theatre Games demonstrate individual and group understanding of the concept being explored.

Each player is encouraged to:

- Explore different perspectives and behaviors
- Balance internal/external focus
- Move beyond memorization to embodied learning
- Support other players&rsquo; explorations
