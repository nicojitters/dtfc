## Preparation

- Open space large enough for pairs to stand facing each other at arm’s length.
- No music needed; silence helps players focus.

## Facilitation

1. **Mirror the Facilitator:** Facilitator shows a small movement with one hand. Group mirrors the movement back. Facilitator progresses to moving both hands in random ways; group mirrors back.
2. **Mirror Each Other:** Divide group into pairs, labeling one player A and the other B.
3. Pairs face each other and touch fingertips lightly — or keep fingertips a few inches apart if preferred.
4. B partner starts by moving one hand; A mirrors back. B progresses to both hands.
5. Switch: A leads and B follows. Give sufficient time for each direction.
6. After a good time of switching, encourage partners to move toward not knowing who is leading and who is following — the goal is seamless, shared movement.

## Evaluation

- Were participants able to follow the movement accurately?
- Did the partners move in ways that show attentiveness to the other?
- What happened when neither partner was designated as leader? Did movement stop, or flow?
- Were there restrictions that kept participants from succeeding — and did the facilitator offer useful adaptations?
