## Preparation

- No special space arrangement required; can be done in any formation.
- Facilitator prepares a set of tongue twisters appropriate to the group's age and ability.
- Example tongue twisters:
  - Shy Sarah saw six Swiss wristwatches.
  - The seething sea ceaseth and so suffices us.
  - Red leather, yellow leather.
  - Big bad bug's blood.
  - Tip of the tongue, and the teeth, and the lips.

## Facilitation

1. Facilitator demonstrates the first tongue twister, reminding players to pronounce consonants — especially at the ends of words.
2. Group repeats each tongue twister several times together.
3. **Relay format:** Facilitator assigns a different tongue twister to each small group of three. Each group:
   - Practices their sentence for clarity.
   - Memorizes it.
   - Moves around the room saying the sentence to others, as if in ordinary conversation.
4. Players can then say their twister at different speeds: very slowly, then faster and faster until it trips — then slow back down.
5. Optional challenge: say the twister in one breath; have a partner close their eyes and give a thumbs up/down on whether they could understand each word.

## Evaluation

- Were words clearly pronounced in place every time?
- Can the line be delivered as a single flowing piece?
- Did confidence in speaking aloud increase over the exercise?
- Are players having fun?
