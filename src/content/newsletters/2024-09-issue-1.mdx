## In this issue

Welcome to the first newsletter from Developmental Theatre: Fearless Creativity! We’re excited to share what we’ve been working on, celebrate our first fundraising milestone, and introduce you to the heart of what we do.

## Highlights

**What is Developmental Theatre?**

Using theatre techniques to develop well-rounded and whole human beings fearless in their creativity. Our values are represented in everything we offer. Our acclaimed reputation and experience with multifaceted approaches to learning celebrates:

- Being a fun, vital, fulfilling intersection of education and the arts
- Unlocking potential by encouraging expanding and exploring individual skills in a supportive, collaborative group environment
- Providing daily theatre-based experiments to expand five natural competencies yielding fearless creativity

**Our Five Competencies**

Providing daily theatre-based experiments to help Players expand five natural competencies yielding fearless creativity:

- **Physical Expression:** Learning how and when to move the body from static to fluid expression — Ready to Move!
- **Vocal Expression:** Leaving behind self-imposed limits to find your voice
- **Context Awareness:** Being aware of and responding to when, who, how, and what is happening where you are — Respect Others
- **Risk Assessment and Management:** Exploring what risks are part of creating a decision to Say Yes And…
- **Resilience:** Practice helps you find what works best for you — Just Do It again, while remembering that learning defeats failure

## Announcements

- 10% to our fiscal sponsor, We Tell Stories ($475.49)

Thank you to every single one of our forty-two founding champions!
