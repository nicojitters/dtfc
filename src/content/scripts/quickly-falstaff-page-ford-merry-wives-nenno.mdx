## The scene

**MISTRESS QUICKLY.**
Marry, this is the short and the long of it: you have
brought her into such a canaries as &rsquo;tis wonderful. The best
courtier of them all, when the court lay at Windsor, could
never have brought her to such a canary. Yet there has been
knights, and lords, and gentlemen, with their coaches; I
warrant you, coach after coach, letter after letter, gift
after gift; smelling so sweetly, all musk; and so rushling,
I warrant you, in silk and gold; and in such alligant terms;
and in such wine and sugar of the best and the fairest, that
would have won any woman&rsquo;s heart. ….

**FALSTAFF.**
This is well; heaven&rsquo;s blessing on your sweet face! ….
I love thee; help me hence. Let me see thee off.

**MISTRESS QUICKLY.**
Will you go, Master Ford?

**FORD (as BROOK).**
I would your worship would grant me that.

**FALSTAFF.**
Master Brook, thou shalt know I will predominate over
the peasant, and thou shalt lie with his wife. Come to me soon
at night: Ford&rsquo;s a knave, and I will aggravate his stile; thou,
Master Brook, shalt know him for knave and cuckold. Come to me
soon at night.

[Exit FALSTAFF.]

**FORD.**
What a damn&rsquo;d Epicurean rascal is this! My heart is ready to
crack with impatience. Who says this is improvident jealousy?
My wife hath sent to him; the hour is fix&rsquo;d; the match is
made. Would any man have thought this? ….
God be prais&rsquo;d for my jealousy! Eleven o&rsquo;clock the hour;
I will prevent this, detect my wife, be reveng&rsquo;d on Falstaff,
and laugh at Ford. ….

[Exit FORD.]

**MISTRESS QUICKLY.**
[To ROBIN] Hark you, sirrah — carry this letter to Master
Fenton; take him this quickly, lest he have too much on his
plate. Make haste!

**ROBIN.**
I will, mistress.

[Exit ROBIN.]
