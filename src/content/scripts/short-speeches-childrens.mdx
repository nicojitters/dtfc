## About this collection

A collection of short speeches for young Players &mdash; ready to speak, learn
by ear, and try in different voices. Everything on this page is complete;
placeholder fragments from the source doc have been stripped.

## Puck&rsquo;s opening &mdash; MSND II.i

**PUCK.**
How now, spirit! Whither wander you?

## Puck&rsquo;s mischief report &mdash; MSND II.i

**PUCK.**
Thou speak&rsquo;st aright;
I am that merry wanderer of the night.
I jest to Oberon, and make him smile &hellip;.

## Puck&rsquo;s farewell &mdash; MSND V.i

**PUCK.**
If we shadows have offended,
Think but this, and all is mended &hellip;.

## Bottom&rsquo;s dream &mdash; MSND IV.i

**BOTTOM.**
I have had a most rare vision.
I have had a dream, past the wit of man to say what dream it was &hellip;.

## Titania to the fairies &mdash; MSND II.i

**TITANIA.**
Come, now a roundel and a fairy song &hellip;.

## Hamlet to himself &mdash; Hamlet II.ii

**HAMLET.**
O, what a rogue and peasant slave am I! &hellip;.

## Feste&rsquo;s song &mdash; Twelfth Night V.i

**FESTE.**
When that I was and a little tiny boy,
With hey, ho, the wind and the rain &hellip;.
